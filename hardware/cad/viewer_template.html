This is the clock's real firmware, src/main.cpp, compiled to WebAssembly. It draws here and on the model's screen, drives the lamp in the glass and plays the sleep sounds. On the model, click the screen to use it, the glass to switch the lamp, the speaker for sleep sounds and the top button to snooze. Jump to moves the clock's time: Alarm turns the alarm on and sets the clock 10 seconds before it. The Serial box takes the same commands as the real clock's USB console; so does clock.command() in the browser console. Settings are kept in this browser, the way the clock keeps them through a power cut.
